Delete your Art Block account

Last updated: June 13, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Art Block account and the personal data we store for you on our servers. Art Block is developed and published by Vardar Studio Inc. (Quebec, Canada).

Art Block is local-first: much of your practice journal lives on your device. Requesting account deletion removes your account and the cloud copy of your data from our backend. Data that exists only on your device is not deleted remotely; you can remove the app or clear local data on your phone separately.

How to request account deletion

To delete your account, send an email from the address associated with your Art Block account:

  1. Email contact@vardarstudio.com
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion
  3. In the message, include the email address you use to sign in to Art Block (and your username, if you have one). If you signed in with Apple, include the email Apple shares with Art Block or note that you use Sign in with Apple.

We may reply to confirm your identity before processing the request. Once verified, we will delete your account and associated server data, usually within 30 days.

Data that is deleted

When your account is deleted, we delete or de-identify the following data stored on our servers (Supabase), where applicable to your account:

  • Your account and authentication record
  • Your email address (for email sign-in accounts)
  • Your cloud backup (practice schedule, session logs, notes, and related journal data)
  • Your social profile (username, bio, avatar)
  • Shared sessions, likes, comments, follows, and groups tied to your account
  • Cover photos and other files you uploaded to our storage for shared sessions
  • Notifications and activity rows associated with your account
  • Block and mute lists associated with your account

Data that may be retained

Some information may be kept after account deletion where reasonably necessary:

  • Moderation records — such as abuse reports you filed or that were filed against you — may be retained for safety, abuse prevention, or legal compliance.
  • Subscription and purchase records processed through Apple or Google may be retained by those platforms and by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) according to their own policies and legal requirements. Deleting your Art Block account does not cancel an active store subscription; cancel through the App Store or Google Play if needed.
  • Backups and logs held by our service providers may persist for a limited period in automated systems before being overwritten.

Deleting data without deleting your account

You can delete individual shared sessions, likes, comments, follows, and groups inside the Art Block app without closing your account. Signing out clears local practice data on your device; your cloud backup remains until you delete it or request full account deletion.

Questions

For privacy questions or to exercise other rights described in our Privacy Policy, contact contact@vardarstudio.com.
