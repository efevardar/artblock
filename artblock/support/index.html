Support

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Need help with Art Block? We’re here for questions about the app, your account, subscriptions, and privacy or data requests. Art Block is developed and published by Vardar Studio Inc. (Quebec, Canada).

Contact us

The fastest way to reach us is by email. Please include your App Store or Google Play account email if your question is about a purchase or account.

  • Email: contact@vardarstudio.com
  • Studio: vardarstudio.com

We aim to respond within a few business days. You can also use the developer contact channel on the Apple App Store or Google Play listing for Art Block.

Common requests

  • Delete your data or account — follow the steps on our account management page (also available in the app under Profile → Settings).
  • Subscriptions & billing — Art Block Pro is managed through Apple or Google. To cancel or change a subscription, use your device’s subscription settings (iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions; Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions). Email us if you need help locating a purchase.
  • Privacy questions — see our Privacy Policy, or email us with your request.
  • Terms of Use — Terms of Use

Bug reports & feedback

Found a bug or have an idea to improve Art Block? Email contact@vardarstudio.com with a short description, your device model, and iOS or Android version. Screenshots help a lot.
