Hi! Thanks for checking Art Block.

I'm Efe, the creator of Art Block and an artist working in the video game industry for over 8 years.

The idea for Art Block came from a workout tracker I've been using for the past year. I'd never been able to stay consistent at the gym before, but that app made consistency feel surprisingly easy.

It made me realize how much my creative practice was missing that same kind of support system. Art school had once provided that rhythm, but over the years I lost a lot of that structure in my day-to-day practice.

So I built Art Block as a tool for myself; something to help me stay accountable, organized, and motivated over time. It's been genuinely helpful for me, and I'm sharing it in the hope that it might help other artists too.

Feel free to reach out with any suggestions on how I can improve the app!

-Efe
